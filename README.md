# saudi-labor-rag

## Day 3 — Model Quality, Retrieval Tuning & Guardrails

During this phase, we focused on improving retrieval quality, generation reliability, and safety. For embeddings, we compared `BAAI/bge-m3`, `intfloat/multilingual-e5-large`, and `Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B`. BGE-M3 was selected because it achieved strong Arabic legal retrieval performance, including 100% Recall@3, while Qwen embeddings were heavier locally. For generation, we first tested `Qwen3-4B Q4_K_M` with llama.cpp, but it occasionally altered important legal numbers and article identifiers, which is unacceptable for legal RAG. We therefore switched to `Gemma 3 4B IT Q4_K_M`, which preserved retrieved legal values more reliably.

The initial RAG used Top-1 dense retrieval, but some correct legal articles appeared only at rank 2 or 3. To solve this, we upgraded the pipeline to `Top-3 retrieval + BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3`, then passed only the best reranked article to Gemma. This successfully recovered cases such as Article 83, which was originally ranked third by dense retrieval but became first after reranking. We also moved article citations outside the LLM and made them deterministic from the retriever to avoid invented legal references.

A major challenge was confidence handling. We first tried sigmoid-based reranker confidence and fixed score margins, but found that reranker scores are not calibrated probabilities and that related legal articles can naturally have small score gaps. We therefore replaced this with a three-zone safety policy: very low scores are rejected, medium scores enter a gray zone where direct legal answers are blocked, and high scores are allowed to proceed. This reflects the project’s main safety principle: it is better to abstain than to answer from the wrong legal provision.

The system was evaluated on 45 Arabic questions covering factual labor-law queries, clarification cases, and out-of-domain questions. The latest results reached **97.37% retrieval accuracy**, **96.67% answer behavior accuracy**, **85.71% out-of-scope accuracy**, and **0 answers based on the wrong article**. The main remaining weakness is no longer retrieval, but answer completeness: Gemma can sometimes retrieve the correct article yet omit one or more legal conditions. The next step is therefore to improve answer completeness and add a verification layer before returning the final response.
